# BeatBound

## Playable Core Gameplay Prototype

**Unity Version:** Unity 6000.3.25f1 BeatBound is a 3D rhythm-platforming game prototype where the player navigates a series of platforms that react to a repeating musical beat. The goal is to time movement and jumps with the rhythm and reach the yellow goal platform. 

## How to Play
**Controls**
+ **WASD** - Move
+ **Space** - Jump

**Objective**

Reach the yellow goal platform at the end of the level. 
The green and red platforms change state according to the beat:
+ **Green** - Active and can be landed on
+ **Red** - Inactive and cannot be landed on

Watch the **BEAT** indicator in the top-right corner to help time your movement. If you fall, you will respawn at the starting point.
